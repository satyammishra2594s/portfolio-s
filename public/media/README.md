# Portfolio media pack

The UI is wired for real stock footage. Put locally downloaded, license-checked assets in this folder with these names:

- `sky.mp4` — night-sky timelapse
- `fog.mp4` — mountain/fog footage
- `fire.mp4` — forest-fire footage
- `sky-poster.jpg`
- `fog-poster.jpg`
- `fire-poster.jpg`
- `gate-closed.jpg`
- `gate-open.jpg`
- `samurai.jpg`

Suggested source pages checked during implementation:

- Pexels forest fire, free to use: https://www.pexels.com/video/forest-fire-with-controlled-burn-in-progress-29961570/
- Pexels aerial forest fire, free to use: https://www.pexels.com/video/aerial-view-of-forest-fire-with-smoke-and-flames-31130038/
- Pexels night sky timelapse, free to use: https://www.pexels.com/video/night-sky-timelapse-with-stars-37083112/
- Pexels fog over mountains, free to use: https://www.pexels.com/video/fog-over-mountains-13830670/
- Unsplash torii gate reference: https://unsplash.com/photos/torii-gate-at-illuminated-japanese-shrine-faCWh5icxtI

For production, prefer downloading the shortest suitable HD clips, trimming them, and transcoding them locally to small MP4/WebM files. Keep combined video payload under about 15 MB.
